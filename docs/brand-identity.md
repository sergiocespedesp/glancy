# Brand identity

> Status: implemented on 2026-10-14. Every asset is generated by `tools/make_icon.py`.

## 1. Idea

To *glance* is to take a quick look. The symbol is an eye **glancing sideways** whose pupil is a thumbnail (a rounded
square). A quick sideways look, never a fixed stare, so it reads as curiosity rather than surveillance: Glancy opens the
user's files and must not look like it is watching them.

Personality: a **creative tool** for people who work with PSD, AI, EPS and RAW files. Vivid color and character,
without being childish.

## 2. Symbol

Two drawings: a full version on a 100 × 100 grid and a small version on a 16 × 16 grid.

| Element | Full version (≥ 20 px) | 16 px version |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Rounded square `x 4, y 4, 92 × 92, radius 22`, coral | `x 0.5, y 0.5, 15 × 15, radius 3.5`, coral |
| Eye | White almond: `M12 50 Q50 12 88 50 Q50 88 12 50 Z` | `M1.5 8 Q8 1.5 14.5 8 Q8 14.5 1.5 8 Z` |
| Pupil | Rounded square `x 50, y 38, 25 × 24, radius 7`, indigo (offset to the right: a sideways look) | `x 8, y 5.5, 5 × 5, radius 1.3`, indigo |
| Glint | Square `x 62, y 42, 8 × 8, radius 2`, amber | No glint |

The 16 px version is used for the 16 px frame of the `.ico` and the 16 px PNG. Both are rasterized with anti-aliasing
(supersampling), with no outlines or shadows.

## 3. Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Coral | `#FF5E62` | Symbol background and brand surfaces. **Never** behind small white text (contrast ≈ 3:1). |
| Indigo | `#2E2470` | Pupil; logo text on light backgrounds; text on light brand surfaces. |
| Amber | `#FFD166` | Pupil glint and occasional accents. Never for text. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Eye; logo text on dark backgrounds. |

Reference dark background for brand pieces (reversed logo, banners): `#1C1A33`.

The app UI does **not** adopt the palette: controls, focus and selection keep the Windows accent color ([design-system.md](design-system.md), Visual principles).
The brand lives in the icon, the Home hero, the About page, notifications and the installer.

## 4. Logo

* Symbol + "Glancy" in **Sora SemiBold (600)**, capitalized, tracking −2 %.
* Cap height of the text = 52 % of the symbol height; gap between symbol and text = 25 % of the symbol height; text
  vertically centerd on the symbol.
* Two versions: **light** (indigo text, for light backgrounds) and **dark** (white text, for dark backgrounds).
* Clear space around it: half the symbol height.
* In the SVG files the text is converted to outlines (no dependency on Sora being installed).
* Minimum sizes: symbol 16 px (using the small version); logo 24 px tall.

**Don't:** recolor the symbol, center the pupil (a fixed stare), rotate or distort the eye, add shadows or gradients,
place the logo on photos or on coral backgrounds, or write "glancy" in lowercase or in another typeface.

## 6. Brand files (`docs/brand/`)

`glancy-symbol.svg`, `glancy-symbol-16.svg`, `glancy-logo-light.svg`, `glancy-logo-dark.svg` and their PNGs (symbol at
512 px; logos 1024 px wide).

Setup wizard images (`installer/images/`, BMP at 100–250 % display scale): `wizard-*.bmp`, the welcome and finish
pages' side panel (164 × 314 at 100 %), the symbol in its upper third on the dark reference background `#1C1A33`;
`wizard-small-*.bmp`, the header of the other pages (55 × 55), the symbol on white.

## 7. Generation

`tools/make_icon.py` (rewritten) defines the geometry once and generates everything: the SVGs, the `.ico`, the app PNGs,
the logos and the setup wizard images. Development dependencies: Pillow (already used), `fonttools` (converts "Glancy" to outlines and
instantiates the variable font) and `Sora[wght].ttf` from the official `google/fonts` repository (`ofl/sora`), pinned by
SHA-256. The variable font is instanced at weight 600 with `fontTools.varLib.instancer`, both for the app and for the
outlines.
